Here is our Brazilian Pudim recipe:

Pudim (specifically Pudim de Leite Condensado) is a classic, beloved Brazilian dessert famous for its ultra-smooth, creamy texture and rich, comforting flavor.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup white granulated sugar
  • 1/3 cup water
  • 1 can (14 oz) sweetened condensed milk
  • 1 can (14 oz) whole milk (use your empty condensed milk can to measure)
  • 3 large eggs
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract (optional)
Steps
  1. Preheat & Prep: Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C). Bring a kettle of water to a boil for your water bath. You will need a 9 or 10-inch tube pan (or a standard flan/bundt mold).
  2. Make the Caramel: In a small saucepan over medium heat, combine the sugar and water. Let it simmer without stirring until it turns a deep, amber color (about 10–15 minutes).
  3. Coat the Pan: Immediately and carefully pour the hot caramel into your baking mold. Swirl the pan to coat the bottom and about an inch up the sides. Set it aside to cool and harden.
  4. Blend the Custard: Add the sweetened condensed milk, whole milk, eggs, and vanilla into a blender. Blend on low for about 30–60 seconds until smooth.
  5. Pour & Strain: To ensure a velvety, hole-free pudim, strain the blended mixture through a fine-mesh sieve directly into your caramelized pan.
  6. Bake in a Water Bath: Place your mold inside a larger roasting pan. Pour boiling water into the roasting pan until it reaches about halfway up the sides of your pudim mold.
  7. Bake: Bake in the oven for about 50 to 75 minutes. The edges should be set, but the center will still have a slight jiggle.
  8. Chill & Serve: Let the pan cool to room temperature, then transfer it to the refrigerator to chill for at least 4 hours (or overnight). To serve, gently warm the bottom of the pan on the stove for a few seconds to loosen the caramel, run a thin knife along the edges, and invert it onto a large rimmed plate.
