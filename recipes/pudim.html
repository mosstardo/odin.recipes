Here is our Brazilian Pudim recipe:

Pudim (specifically Pudim de Leite Condensado) is a classic, beloved Brazilian dessert famous for its ultra-smooth, creamy texture and rich, comforting flavor.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup white granulated sugar
  • 1/3 cup water
  • 1 can (14 oz) sweetened condensed milk
  • 1 can (14 oz) whole milk (use your empty condensed milk can to measure)
  • 3 large eggs
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract (optional)
