Here is our Brazilian Pudim recipe:

Home Page

    Ingredients

  1. Bread: 1 French roll (pão francês) or Ciabatta
  2. Meat: 150g of beef steak (like skirt, sirloin, or ribeye), cut into thin strips
  3. Cheese: 2–3 slices of Mozzarella or Provolone cheese
  4. Aromatics: ½ white onion (thinly sliced) and 1 minced garlic clove
  5. Fat & Seasoning: 1 tablespoon of butter, salt, and black pepper to taste
