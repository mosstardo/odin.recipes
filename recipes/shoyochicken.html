Here is our Shoyo Chicken recipe:

Hawaiian Shoyu Chicken is the ultimate sweet-and-savory comfort food, featuring chicken thighs braised until fall-apart tender in a rich, glossy glaze of soy sauce, brown sugar, ginger, and garlic.

Ingredients:

  • Chicken Thighs: 2 to 3 pounds (bone-in, skin-on works best).
  • Soy Sauce (Shoyu): 1 cup.
  • Brown Sugar: 1 cup (packed).
  • Water: 1 cup.
  • Fresh Ginger: One 2-inch piece (sliced)
  • Garlic: 4 to 5 cloves (smashed).
  • Green Onions: 4 stalks (chopped).

Steps:

  1. Combine the Sauce: In a large, heavy-bottomed pot or Dutch oven, whisk together the soy sauce, water, and brown sugar until the sugar mostly dissolves.
  2. Add Aromatics: Add the smashed garlic, sliced ginger, and the white parts of the green onions into the pot.
  3. Poach the Chicken: Nestle the chicken thighs into the pot, ensuring the liquid covers them as much as possible.
  4. Simmer: Bring the liquid to a rolling boil over high heat. Once boiling, immediately reduce the heat to low, cover the pot with a lid, and let it simmer for 45 to 60 minutes.
  5. Serve: Carefully remove the tender chicken to a serving platter. You can skim off any excess fat from the sauce and ladle a little over the chicken. Garnish with the fresh, chopped green onion greens.
