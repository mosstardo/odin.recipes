Here is our sandwich recipe:

This classic sanduba is a comforting street-style sandwich featuring thinly sliced, juicy beef steak seared with garlic and tossed with caramelized onions. It is topped with a generous layer of melted, gooey cheese and served inside a perfectly toasted, crusty bread roll.

Ingredients

  • Bread: 1 French roll (pão francês) or Ciabatta
  • Meat: 150g of beef steak (like skirt, sirloin, or ribeye), cut into thin strips
  • Cheese: 2–3 slices of Mozzarella or Provolone cheese
  • Aromatics: ½ white onion (thinly sliced) and 1 minced garlic clove
  • Fat & Seasoning: 1 tablespoon of butter, salt, and black pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Prep Your Base: Place your two slices of bread flat on a clean cutting board or plate.
  2. Apply Condiments: Spread a thin, even layer of mayonnaise, mustard, or butter on one side of both slices of bread. This adds flavor and creates a moisture barrier so your bread does not get soggy.
  3. Layer the Cheese: Place your cheese slices onto the bottom piece of bread. Putting the cheese first helps protect the bread from wet ingredients.
  4. Fold the Meat: Do not lay the deli meat completely flat. Instead, slightly fold or wrinkle each slice to create volume and texture, making the sandwich fluffier.
  5. Add the Veggies: Place your lettuce, tomato slices, and onion rings on top of the meat.
  6. Season: Sprinkle a tiny pinch of salt and freshly cracked black pepper directly onto the tomatoes or vegetables to make their flavors pop.
  7. Close and Press: Place the second slice of bread (sauce-side down) on top. Press down very gently with your hand to bond the layers together.
  8. Cut and Serve: Use a sharp, serrated knife to cut the sandwich diagonally into two triangles. Serving with a side of potato chips or a pickle is optional but highly recommended.
